### Abstract

We study the three-dimensional structure of the pion beyond the leading Fock state by including a dynamical gluon, |π⟩=|qq̄⟩+|qq̄ g⟩. Within the light-cone quark model, we calculate the pion GPDs, TMDs, and PDFs from light-front wave function overlaps, using a modified Brodsky--Huang--Lepage wave function with the Melosh--Wigner rotation for the valence sector and the light-front quark--gluon vertex for the qq̄ g sector. The quark-number and momentum sum rules are satisfied without additional constraints. We further obtain the electromagnetic form factor and the mass decomposition. After NLO evolution, we compare the PDFs with pion-induced Drell--Yan, J/ψ, Υ, and HERA leading-neutron F₂^π data. The qq̄ g sector contributes little to Drell--Yan but becomes important for gluon-sensitive observables such as J/ψ production and the pion structure function at small x
