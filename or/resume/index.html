Academic Background

A journey from a small village in Odisha to the frontiers of High Energy Physics research across India, Japan, Switzerland, and Russia.

  1. 2022–2026

    Ph.D. in Computational High Energy Physics

    Thesis: Tomography of Light and Heavy Mesons Using Light-front Dynamics. Supervisor: Dr. Harleen Dahiya. Successfully defended on 2nd April, 2026. Courses: Particle Physics, Adv. Particle Physics, QFT, Research Methodology, Exp. HEP. CGPA: 8.75/10.0

  2. Jun–Sep 2025

    Visiting Fellow — JINR, Dubna, Russia

    Project: Extraction of hadron GPDs using neural networks and energy-momentum tensor calculations. Supervisor: Prof. Oleg V. Teryaev. Joint Institute for Nuclear Research (16 Jun – 12 Sep 2025).

  3. Feb 2025

    Visiting Fellow — IIT Bhilai, Chhattisgarh

    Worked on applications of NJL model to understand hadron structure. Supervisor: Prof. Sabyasachi Ghosh (16–23 Feb 2025).

  4. 2019–2021

    M.Sc. in Physics — NIT Jamshedpur

    Project: Proton-Oxygen Elastic Scattering by Taylor Series Expansion. Supervisor: Prof. Ujjwal Laha. CGPA: 8.59/10.0

  5. 2016–2019

    B.Sc. in Physics — Bhadrak Autonomous College

    Project: Superconductivity and its Application to Maglev Trains. Supervisor: Dr. Rajat Kumar Pradhan. CGPA: 8.91/10.0

  6. 2015–2016

    Higher Secondary (12th) — Siddhivinayak +2 Science College

    2nd Topper in Physics in CHSE Final Examination, Odisha. Marks: 478/600 (78%). Balasore, Odisha.

  1. 2026–Present

    Postdoctoral Researcher — Institute of Physics, Academia Sinica

    Conducting research in theoretical high energy physics at the Institute of Physics, Academia Sinica, Taipei, Taiwan. Continuing work on hadron tomography, meson structure, and QCD phenomenology.

  2. Apr 2026

    Seminar at Prof. C. R. Ji's Group — Extraction of Pion GPDs (NCSU, USA)

    Presented 'Extraction of Pion GPDs' via Zoom from IOP, Academia Sinica. Discussed data-driven extraction of pion unpolarized quark GPDs, form factor fitting, and impact-parameter-space interpretations.

  3. Apr–May 2026

    Workshop — EIC-Asia Workshop on QCD and Hadron Structure, Academia Sinica

    Attended the EIC-Asia Workshop on QCD and Hadron Structure at the Institute of Physics, Academia Sinica, Taipei. Topics included lattice QCD, meson and nucleon structures at EIC.

  4. 2022–2026

    Senior Research Fellow — NIT Jalandhar

    Hadron tomography: TMDs, GPDs, GTMDs, GDAs, form factors, PDFs of mesons in light-front dynamics. Published 29 papers in top journals: PRD, JHEP, PLB, NPA, NPB, PTEP, EPJP, CPC, EPJA.

  5. Jan 2025

    Premier Research Award — NIT Jalandhar

    Received the Premier Research Award in the Department of Physics, NIT Jalandhar on 26th January 2025.

  6. Aug 2025

    Conference Talk — JINR-BLTP 2025, Dubna

    Presented 'Gravitational Form Factors of Pion' at the 'Interplay of Gravity and Strong Interactions' conference, BLTP, JINR.

  7. Jul 2025

    Seminar at Prof. C. R. Ji's Group (NCSU, USA)

    Presented 'Internal Structure of Light Mesons through Distribution Functions' via Zoom. Discussed PDFs, GPDs, TMDs and mechanical properties.

  8. Mar 2025

    Seminar at JINR, Dubna, Russia

    Presented 'Internal structure of pion, kaon, and ρ meson in light front dynamics' covering TMDs, GPDs, FFs, PDFs, and medium modifications.

  9. Dec 2024

    Conference Talk — DAE SNP 2024, IIT Roorkee

    Presented 'Spin-1 unpolarized GPDs in Light-front formalism' at the 68th DAE Symposium on Nuclear Physics.

  10. Oct–Nov 2024

    Conference Talk — HHIQCD 2024, Kyoto, Japan

    Presented 'Structural analysis of Pion and Kaon with distribution functions' at Yukawa Institute for Theoretical Physics, Kyoto University.

  11. Aug 2024

    PSI Summer School 2024 — Zuoz, Switzerland

    Attended 'From Low to High: Particle Physics at the Frontier' at Lyceum Alpinum, Zuoz, Switzerland (Aug 4–10, 2024).

  12. Jan–Feb 2024

    Conference Talk — ICTS-TIFR 2024, Bengaluru

    Presented 'Structural analysis of pion with distribution functions (TMDs, GPDs and GDAs)' at the EIC workshop, ICTS-TIFR, Bengaluru.
