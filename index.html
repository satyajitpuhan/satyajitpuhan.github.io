📄 New preprint!

Title: Extraction of Pion Unpolarized Quark and Gluon Generalized Parton Distributions using Deep Neural-Networks Authors: S. Puhan, S. Sharma, N. Kumar Published in: e-Print: 2608.07085 [hep-ph] e-Print: 2608.07085 [hep-ph] INSPIRE-HEP: record 3188657

Abstract

We present a deep neural-network (DNN) extraction of the pion unpolarized quark and gluon generalized parton distributions (GPDs) using the corresponding parton distribution functions (PDFs) from the JAM21 and xFitter analysis, together with experimental measurements of the pion electromagnetic form factor (EMFF) and lattice quantum chromodynamics (QCD) results. The GPDs are parameterized using a physics-informed neural-network (PINN) that incorporates the known PDF behavior, an exponential momentum-transfer dependence, and a trainable neural network (NN) component. The network parameters are determined by minimizing a χ^2-based loss function. For the valence-quark GPDs, the loss function includes contributions from the EMFF, squared EMFF, charge-normalization constraints, and regularization terms. For the gluon GPDs, it incorporates constraints from the gluon gravitational form factors together with regularization. This framework enables a flexible, nonparametric extraction while preserving the essential theoretical and phenomenological constraints. By employing the full ensemble of available PDF replicas, we quantify the uncertainties of the extracted GPDs over a broad kinematic range in the longitudinal momentum fraction and momentum transfer, with the uncertainty bands corresponding to the 1σ confidence interval. The extracted valence-quark GPDs are found to be in good agreement with available lattice-QCD calculations. Our study demonstrates that DNN-based methods provide a flexible and robust framework for extracting pion GPDs and probing the multidimensional internal structure of the pion, offering a promising avenue for future investigations of hadron tomography

Analysis on the parton distribution functions of heavy mesons

In this work, we investigate the constituent parton distribution functions (PDFs) of the kaon and heavy pseudoscalar mesons within the light-cone quark model.

Details

🎉 New publication!

Title: Analysis on the parton distribution functions of heavy mesons Authors: S. Puhan Published in: Phys. Rev. D 114 (2026) e-Print: 2605.17389 [hep-ph] INSPIRE-HEP: record 3157139 DOI: 10.1103/6gjf-24ll

Abstract

In this work, we investigate the constituent parton distribution functions (PDFs) of the kaon and heavy pseudoscalar mesons within the light-cone quark model. Starting from the initial-scale quark and antiquark PDFs, obtained by evaluating the quark-quark correlation functions for individual mesons, we perform QCD evolution to determine their partonic structure at higher energy scales. The QCD evolution is carried out using the next-to-leading-order (NLO) Dokshitzer-Gribov-Lipatov-Altarelli-Parisi equations. We further compute the average longitudinal momentum fractions carried by the individual constituents at both the model and evolved scales. In addition, we predict the NLO structure functions of the kaon at energy scales relevant to the upcoming Electron-Ion Collider (EIC). For the COMPASS++/AMBER experiment, we also present detailed predictions for the NLO Drell-Yan cross sections induced by both K+ and K- beams, using carbon, tungsten, and aluminum as nuclear targets. Finally, we demonstrate the dominance of the heavier constituents over the lighter constituents in heavy mesons in terms of the momentum fractions they carry

Pion parton distribution functions in the light-cone quark model and experimental constraints

In this work, we investigate the valence quark parton distribution functions (PDFs) of the pion within the light-cone quark model.

Details

🎉 New publication!

Title: Pion parton distribution functions in the light-cone quark model and experimental constraints Authors: H. G. P, S. Puhan, A. K. P., R. Pandey, H. Dahiya, A. Kumar, S. Dutt Published in: Phys. Rev. D 114 (2026) e-Print: 2604.05762 [hep-ph] INSPIRE-HEP: record 3140657 DOI: 10.1103/q5rc-999c

Abstract

In this work, we investigate the valence quark parton distribution functions (PDFs) of the pion within the light-cone quark model. The initial quark PDFs are calculated by solving the quark-quark correlation function for the pseudoscalar mesons. The initial quark PDFs have been evolved to higher energy scales through the Dokshitzer-Gribov-Lipatov-Altarelli-Parisi (DGLAP) evolution equations. We also find that our calculated evolved PDFs match experimental and available theoretical extraction data. For the first time, we have also predicted the F2 structure function at next-to-leading (NLO) order accuracy. The calculated F2 structure function has been compared with the available ZEUS and H1 experimental data at DESY-HERA over a wide range of energy scales. Additionally, we display the forward pion production cross-section for the Drell-Yan process caused by pions using the pion PDFs that were calculated and the target nucleon PDFs from the LHAPDF nucleus datasets. The evolved F2 structure function of the pion has been studied at the upcoming electron-ion collider energy kinematics. Overall, it was observed that the quark PDFs of pions computed using the light-cone quark model are consistent with the experimental results

Key Highlights

  • 🔹 First projection for constraining moderate-to-large-x gluon helicity distribution Δg(x) via J/ψ production at NICA-SPD energy

From Humble Beginnings to the World of Physics

I am Satyajit Puhan, born on 15th May 1999 in a small village called Chhatara, Srirampur, Balasore – 756029, Odisha, India. Growing up in a simple family of farmers, I learned the true meaning of resilience and humility through life's early challenges.

I also worked as a Visiting Fellow at JINR, Dubna, Moscow under Prof. Oleg V. Teryaev, on extraction of hadron GPDs and energy-momentum tensor calculations.

I am currently a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Institute of Physics, Academia Sinica, Taipei, Taiwan, where I continue my work on hadron tomography, meson structure, and QCD phenomenology.

I have published 42 papers in leading journals including Phys. Rev. D, JHEP, Phys. Lett. B, Nucl. Phys. A/B, PTEP, Eur. Phys. J. A/Plus, and Chin. Phys. C, and presented my work internationally at Kyoto (Japan), Zuoz (Switzerland), Dubna (Russia), TIFR (Mumbai), IIT Roorkee, and ICTS Bengaluru.

What I am today — and whatever I strive to become tomorrow — is because of the people who stood by me through every hardship.

What I Do

Research Areas

My primary research focuses on understanding the internal structure of hadrons through modern theoretical frameworks in light-front dynamics.

1 / 4

Transverse Momentum Distributions (TMDs)

Model-dependent leading-twist and subleading-twist TMDs for spin-0 (pion, kaon), spin-1/2 (proton), and spin-1 (ρ meson, heavy vector mesons, photon) hadrons within the light-front quark model. Includes extraction of T-even and T-odd TMDs, Boer–Mulders and Sivers functions, spin densities in transverse momentum space, Wigner distributions, and higher-twist PDFs. Positivity bounds and Lorentz-invariance relations are systematically examined.

2 / 4

Generalized Parton Distributions (GPDs)

GPDs for spin-0 (pion, kaon) and spin-1 (ρ meson) hadrons encoding both spatial and momentum correlations. Extraction of electromagnetic (Dirac, Pauli) and gravitational (energy-momentum tensor) form factors via Ji's sum rule. Generalized distribution amplitudes (GDAs) for pion. Global extraction of pion GPDs from Jefferson Lab charge form factor data using neural networks. Directly relevant for upcoming Electron-Ion Collider (EIC) and JLab 12 GeV measurements.

3 / 4

Hadronic & Mechanical Properties

Spin-orbit correlations, leading-twist and higher-twist distribution amplitudes (DAs) evolved via ERBL equations. Electromagnetic form factors, charge radii, magnetic moments, and quadrupole moments for vector mesons. Decay constants, mass spectra, and radially excited states of quarkonia (charmonium, bottomonium). Radiative transition form factors of charmonia (J/ψ → η_c γ). Gravitational form factors (D-term), pressure and shear force distributions inside the pion revealing mechanical stability.

4 / 4

Medium Modifications & Extraction

In-medium modification of TMDs, PDFs, GPDs, and form factors in symmetric nuclear matter using LFQM combined with the chiral SU(3) coupled quark-meson-Friedberg–Lee (CQMF) model at finite baryon density. Data-driven extraction of pion TMDs and PDFs from E615 and NA10 Drell–Yan cross-section data via neural network parametrization with DGLAP-evolved distributions. Monte Carlo simulations of charmonia (J/ψ, ψ(2S)) production using Pythia 8312 with color-singlet and color-octet NRQCD mechanisms. Gluon helicity distribution Δg(x) constraints via J/ψ production double-spin asymmetry (A_LL) at NICA-SPD energy (√s = 27 GeV). Transport coefficients using the NJL–Polyakov loop model at finite temperature and chemical potential.
